# Development log — 2026-08-13

## Collision requalification candidate

TODO 1 resumed after play testing showed that actor and blocking-decoration
contact distances were roughly twice as large as desired while the accepted
wall and moving-door behavior remained sound.

The candidate implementation keeps the 512-byte blocking-static bitset as the
common-path broad phase and builds an 8 KiB dense `uint16` tile-to-static table
once per level attempt. An empty lookup entry is zero; a blocking tile stores
its stable static slot plus one. Only a bitset hit resolves the table entry and
tests the player's center against an inclusive `0x6000` square around the
centered static. Normal movement therefore does not scan the static list.

Player-to-shootable-actor and actor-to-player physical contact now use the same
inclusive `0x6000` center-square threshold. The accepted `0x4800` player
wall/moving-door radius, pickup reach, aiming, and behavioral ranges remain
separate. The patrol regression also exposed an older coupling in the fully
open door close-start guard: adjacent actors must retain original Wolf3D's
`0x5800` `MINDIST` boundary. That actor-only rule now has its own bounds helper
and no longer inherits the tuned player radius.

Automated validation before interactive review:

- host collision/oracle suite: 74 tests and 10,124 subtests passed;
- randomized direct-eZ80 ClipMove: 10,027 records passed;
- direct-eZ80 actor suite: 10,494 checks passed;
- direct-eZ80 patrol/door suite: 607 checks passed, including the exact
  `11:57ff` blocked / `11:5800` clear close-start boundary;
- door-use: 19/19; pickups: 31/31; death state: 202/202;
- area connectivity: 1,288 checks across eight cases;
- production application assembled successfully at 105,033 bytes.

Emulator feel-testing and hardware acceptance remain pending. Per project
policy this emulator-dependent candidate is intentionally uncommitted until
human validation.

## Second physical-clearance candidate

The complete `0x6000` build subsequently passed emulator and Agon hardware
play testing: the application ran correctly and actor/static proximity felt
substantially better. The Author requested one further halving of only the
shared physical-object clearance, from `0x6000` to `0x3000`. Wall and moving
door radius (`0x4800`), original actor-only door close-start margin (`0x5800`),
pickup reach, aiming, and dog jump/bite ranges remain unchanged. This smaller
candidate requires fresh automated, emulator, and hardware qualification.

Automated qualification of the `0x3000` candidate passed:

- host collision/oracle suite: 74 tests and 10,124 subtests;
- randomized direct-eZ80 ClipMove: 10,027 records;
- direct-eZ80 actor suite: 10,494 checks;
- direct-eZ80 patrol/door suite: 607 checks;
- door-use: 19/19; pickups: 31/31; death state: 202/202;
- area connectivity: 1,288 checks across eight cases.

The directed static sliding vector was moved to the new exact contact boundary;
its former destination correctly became clear space after the radius reduction.
Interactive emulator and hardware acceptance remain pending.

## Midpoint physical-clearance candidate

Interactive emulator testing rejected `0x3000`: it allowed visible penetration
through broad blocking-static artwork such as tables. The next candidate is the
exact midpoint between the accepted-but-roomy `0x6000` build and the rejected
`0x3000` build, namely `0x4800`. This remains confined to actor/static physical
contact; all independently governed ranges remain unchanged.

The midpoint's host/oracle suite passed 74 tests and 10,124 subtests; direct
eZ80 qualification passed 10,027 randomized ClipMove records, 10,494 actor
checks, and all 607 patrol/door checks. Interactive acceptance remains pending.

Interactive emulator play testing accepted the `0x4800` midpoint: actor
proximity felt correct and broad statics such as tables no longer admitted
visible penetration. The exact deployed binary and all 63 declared runtime
payload files were then copied through the canonical journaled SD deployment
workflow. Source/card hashes matched, `autoexec.txt` remained byte-identical,
and the application passed final play testing on Agon hardware.
