# Wolf3DOrig development log — 2026-11-04

## Source-correct death-music lifecycle

- The residual parity sweep compared `orig/src/WOLFSRC/WL_GAME.C::GameLoop`
  and `Died()` directly with the production death state machine.  DOS calls
  `StopMusic()` before dispatching `ex_died`, then calls `StartMusic()` only
  after a surviving attempt has returned through `SetupGameLevel`.
- The Agon port previously kept the floor IMF sequencer alive during the
  death turn, red fizzle, and acknowledgment hold.  This was a real parity
  defect, independent of the accepted fizzle/render implementation.
- `wolf3d_death.inc` now exposes default-safe music-stop and music-restart
  hooks.  Lethal promotion invokes stop exactly once before requesting
  `PLAYERDEATHSND`; successful attempt reseed invokes restart exactly once
  after canonical inventory/score restoration and before the respawn render.
  Final-life game over and failed reseed do not restart music.
- Production installs `w3d_music_stop` and
  `w3d_music_start_current_floor` at the same common initialization seam used
  for new and loaded games.  The reusable death fixture substitutes counting
  hooks and pins alive, repeated-promotion, respawn, and game-over behavior.

## Completed-floor key lifecycle

- A continuing `WL_GAME.C::GameLoop` comparison found that ordinary and
  secret completion clear `gamestate.keys` before `LevelCompleted` draws its
  status bar.  Production instead left the completed floor's key flags live
  until the destination world seed, so a carried gold or silver key could be
  shown incorrectly throughout the tally.
- `w3d_intermission_begin` now clears the authoritative key flags before it
  composes the retained status bar.  Victory remains unchanged because the
  original `ex_victorious` branch does not pass through this clear or through
  `LevelCompleted`.

## Death-hold audit (no code change)

- DOS follows its 100-tic death hold with `SD_WaitSoundDone`.  The translated
  43-step hold is the exact 30 Hz equivalent of that 100-tic interval, and the
  resident `PLAYERDEATHSND` is 329 ms, well below the roughly 1.43-second hold.
  No additional playback-status wire query or blocking wait is required for
  the current asset contract.
